        Made with open things

        WhiskedAway stands on the shoulders of people who share their work.

        • Gemma's face: Microsoft's Fluent Emoji "woman genie", MIT licence, pixelated by hand for that 1997 feeling.
        • The kitchen's furniture and materials: Poly Haven, CC0. Given to everyone, no strings.
        • The room: built and rendered in Blender, free and open source (GPL).
        • The 360° view: three.js, MIT licence.
        • The window views and concept art: made with ChatGPT for WhiskedAway.
        • Bot protection: Cloudflare Turnstile.

        Copyleft spirit: take what helps you, credit what you borrow, share what you make.
