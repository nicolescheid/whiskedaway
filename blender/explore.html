How it works ⌄

What is WhiskedAway?

WhiskedAway is a recipe site you explore. Instead of a menu, you wander a magic inn by the sea, held inside a balloon whisk. Gemma, the Inn's kitchen genie, notices what you're drawn to and leaves you ideas for what to cook. It's free, there's no account, and it works on phones and computers. Made by Praxilogue in Aotearoa New Zealand.

How the Inn works

  1. Step through the front door.
  2. Look around, and touch whatever catches your eye: the window, the armchair, a jar on the shelf.
  3. Gemma notices, and leaves you an idea that fits.

You don't type anything. She reads where you go and what you touch, and when she asks you something, you answer with a tap. She picks from recipes written for the Inn rather than inventing them. Sound is off until you turn it on.

Ideas and your recipe book

As you wander, Gemma's Ideas rise as small bubbles. Tap one for a closer look: what it is, why she chose it, how long it takes. Keep the ones you like, or set one aside, and she'll learn from both. What you keep goes into your recipe book, with its ingredients and a short method. The book stays on your device until you clear it. Gemma can also write a full recipe for you, up to three a visit.

Your weather, your time, your season

The Inn opens in your own weather, at your own time of day, in your own season.

  • Weather: our server looks up the current weather for the rough area your connection comes from, to about 50 km, using Open-Meteo. Only the area is sent, never anything that identifies you, and nothing about you is kept.
  • Time: the clocks follow your device's own clock.
  • Season: your device's time zone gives a first guess at your hemisphere, and our server can confirm it from the rough area of your connection. Only "north" or "south" comes back, and the server keeps nothing.

Why? Because these change what you feel like eating. Soup when it's raining, something cold when it's hot, porridge in the morning, and whatever's in season.

Change anything

It's your Inn, so it's yours to change. Turn the barometer to change the weather. Push the hands of a clock to cook for another time of day. Turn the seasons wheel in the Conservatory. Pick an energy candle to say how you're feeling. Gemma's ideas change with you, and what you tell her follows you through every room for the visit. Telling her what's in your pantry, where you live and what you can't eat is coming.

Meet Gemma

Hello, love. I'm Gemma, and this is my Inn. I grow the herbs, I light the range, and I know which door sticks. Have a wander. I'll notice what you're drawn to and leave you something to cook. I won't tell you what's good for you, and I won't diagnose anything. I'll just help you find something you feel like eating.

Gemma is an AI kitchen genie. Her suggestions are chosen with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. When it's unavailable, a simpler picker built into the page stands in.

What's kept about you

There's no account, and nothing asks for your name. What you do in the Inn, and what you tell Gemma, is forgotten when you close the tab, apart from small notes so she doesn't repeat herself. To choose ideas, the Inn sends your wander to our server: what you touched and changed, never who you are. Our server asks Claude, Anthropic's AI model, to pick an idea. It doesn't store your wander, and Anthropic handles it under its own data policy. When you ask Gemma for the full recipe, that card and what you've told her this visit, such as dietary needs or what's in your pantry, go to Claude the same way, and our server keeps none of it. The Inn counts visits anonymously, without cookies, and never who. If your browser asks not to be tracked, nothing is counted. Your recipe book stays on your device until you clear it.

Questions

Is WhiskedAway free?

Yes. There's nothing to buy and nothing to sign up for. Step through the door and wander.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no account and no login. Your visit is forgotten when you close the tab. Your recipe book stays on your own device.

Does it know where I am?

Only roughly. To match the Inn's weather to yours, our server looks up the weather for the area your connection comes from, to about 50 km, and keeps nothing about you. It also tells the Inn which country that is, as a starting point for the Map, which you can change. Your season comes from your time zone, checked against that rough area, and only "north" or "south" comes back.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works on phones, tablets and computers. Drag to look around, and tap to touch things.
