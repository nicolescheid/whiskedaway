# Recorded sounds in the Inn

Everything else the Inn plays is synthesised in `blender/explore/sound.js` (CC0, made for WhiskedAway).
These are excerpts, trimmed, faded, loudness-matched and mixed to mono with ffmpeg on 19 Oct 2026.
Each file's licence was checked on its source page on that day. They are credited on the site under
"Made with open things".
