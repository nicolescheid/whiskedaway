## Look and continuity

★ A well-loved old apothecary shop, fitted into the same countryside Inn, for playful chia-pudding experiments. The traditional Chinese herbal-pharmacy influence comes through practical drawer cabinetry, ingredient storage, weighing and wrapping. Keep the source respected: no lantern/dragon decoration, fake calligraphy, costume clichés or themed-set embellishments.

Photographic interiors realism: cream limewash #f4efe4, honey oak beams and wide floorboards, worn red-brown lacquered wood, herb green #5c7c5c, aged brass #b07f52 and deep brown #2c2416. Bright late morning with green-filtered glasshouse daylight, soft lane-window light and warm fridge light. Natural materials, rubbed handles, creased paper/string, a few spilt seeds; not glossy CGI or abandoned-shop grime.

## Motion and sound handoff

The instrument is the clockwork songbird: visitor winds the key, the beak opens/closes with small head/wing/tail movements and it sings. Brass mechanism remains visible; no transformation into a live bird. Keep winding, spring/gear motion and sound trigger separate. Flasks have restrained tiny bubbles and a little warm convection, no neon glow or coloured smoke. Drawer travel and scale pans can respond gently to interaction.

Use the supplied floorplan’s interaction rules: sound begins with visitor action, the shared sound switch controls it, and weather/time changes fade between states. No sound files or rights-cleared recordings are part of this image pack.
