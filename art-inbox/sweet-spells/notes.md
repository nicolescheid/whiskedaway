## Magic effects

- ★ Whisk turns slowly on a subtly tilted axis with a tiny vertical bob, no visible supports or hands. Keep wires separate and avoid a violent vortex.
- ★ Raspberries float individually, gentle 5–15 mm drift, never a swarm. Preserve plausible berry size and real skin detail.
- ★ Chocolate is a thick, continuous, glossy, viscous ribbon leading into the bowl. Use an animated bevelled curve/mesh and subtle surface movement; avoid a rigid metallic corkscrew or flat painted streak. Small droplets may separate naturally.
- Restrained sparse golden particles catch real light; low opacity, few points, no halo ring or fireworks. The effect must not obscure the whisk/food.
- ★ Music-box lid opens about 95–105°. As it lifts, the dancer rises/unfolds and turns slowly around the spindle. Stop/retract before closing. Test collisions with lid and sides.
- ★ Bottle glow: low warm emission through ruby/amber liquids with tiny local practical lights only where needed; preserve translucent material, no self-lit labels.
- ★ Sugar pan emits a thin translucent curl of rose-coloured steam, upward buoyancy with soft diffusion, no dense smoke plume. Small fire flicker and gentle flame movement.
- No magical people, summoned faces, symbols or occult props. The room’s kindness comes from food, light and small impossible motions.
