# Cuddlecore Kitchen — Blender props & materials
Date: 17 October 2026
Project: WhiskedAway

★ = essential to the mood. Dimensions, roughness and light levels below are proposed build starting points, not measurements recovered from the images.

## Look lock
★ A comforting English/Kiwi countryside inn kitchen: photographic cookbook/interiors realism, cream limewash (#f4efe4), honeyed oak, aged brass, deep brown (#2c2416), dusty berry (#8b4d5c). Rainy late afternoon; cool soft window light meets warm candles and a small brass lamp. Imperfect, quietly used materials, creased linen, chips and rubbed wood. The soul is rain, steam and candlelight.

No people, readable text, garbled lettering, logos, watermarks, modern appliances or screens. The sleeping cat is intentional. Keep the scene tactile and uncluttered.

## Structure — walls, beams, floor, window
- ★ Room shell: square 4.5 × 4.5 m internal footprint; proposed 2.7 m ceiling. Uneven cream limewashed plaster over masonry, soft corners and subtle trowel texture.
- ★ Exposed aged oak ceiling beams, approximately 180–220 mm sections. Fine splits and rubbed edges, restrained rather than distressed everywhere.
- ★ Wide timber floorboards, approximately 180–240 mm wide, dark honey/brown, modest gaps, worn footpaths and occasional old nail heads. Avoid exaggerated displacement.
- Cream painted timber skirting, simple door/window trim; chips near contact points.
- ★ Front window: one timber sash, two sashes with three vertical panes each; proposed opening about 1.55 m wide × 1.4 m high, sill about 1.05 m above floor. Match its muntin arrangement throughout the Blender scene.
- ★ Rain on exterior glass: uneven droplets and thin gravity streaks; soft distant wet hills/coast beyond. Keep rain separate from the glass base material.
- ★ Two dusty berry linen curtains on a simple dark rod, gathered and naturally creased; modest translucency, not velvet.
- One old doorway on back wall, approximately 0.9 × 2.05 m, to the inn hallway.

## Lighting — sources, temperature, rough brightness
Keep one frozen rainy afternoon across all cameras. These are art-direction estimates; balance with scene scale, exposure and the chosen render settings.

| Source | Temperature / color | Approximate brightness target | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ Rainy sky through window | 6500–7500 K, cool soft daylight | Dominant broad fill; aim roughly 150–300 lux at front worktop, much less at back wall. | Outside front window; large soft source, no direct sun beam. |
| ★ Three candle flames | 1800–2000 K | About 8–15 lumens per candle; local warm pools, not whole-room flood. | Exact left-counter candle positions; flame emission plus subtle supporting light if required. |
| ★ Brass lamp | 2500–2700 K | Around 150–250 lumens, shielded by brass shade; modest warm pool on counter and pantry edge. | Front-right counter beside pantry. |
| Indirect room bounce | Derived from above sources | Low; preserve soft shadow gradients without crushed black corners. | Cream walls/oak naturally bounce light; avoid unexplained fill lamps. |
| ★ Steam illumination | No independent light or emission | Catch existing window light; readable against mid/dark background. | Above pot rim. |
| Exterior landscape | Same rainy sky | Lower detail/contrast than interior heroes. | Through window, no bright sunset or sun. |

Camera: full-frame 24 mm, 1.6 m eye height, level verticals, about f/4 for wall concepts. Close-ups use the requested shallow depth of field; do not transfer that blur to the final 360 environment. Keep exposure and white balance locked while changing direction.

- Left: shelf and candle counter around x=-1.95, y=+0.65.
- Right: pantry around x=+1.95, y=+1.0; cookbook shelves farther toward back, y around -0.4.
- Back: chair to viewer-left when facing back (world x around +0.9), door to viewer-right (world x around -0.9). Preserve a clear route to door.
