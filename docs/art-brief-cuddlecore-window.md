# WhiskedAway: ChatGPT brief #2, the view through the Cuddlecore window

The rebuilt 3D kitchen looks out through a blank white window. We need the **view outside**:
the wet coastal hills and sea from the concept frames. It becomes a backdrop placed a few metres
behind the window in Blender. The rain streaks on the glass are already done in 3D.

**Use the same ChatGPT conversation as the concept pack** if you still have it, so the look carries
over. If not, start fresh and attach `art-inbox/cuddlecore/01-front-wall.png` as a reference.

Save the rainy view as `art-inbox/cuddlecore/14-window-rain.png`, then make the weather set
below.

---

## The prompt

> Using the same look as the Cuddlecore Kitchen concept frames, make the **view through the
> kitchen window**: what you'd see outside, with no window, frame, glass, curtains or room in shot.
>
> - **Scene:** a rainy late afternoon on a quiet stretch of coast. In the foreground, a wet
>   cottage garden slopes away (low stone wall, rosemary, long grass). In the middle distance,
>   green fields and hedgerows run down to a sheltered bay. Beyond that, grey-blue sea and soft
>   headlands fading into rain. It's the same coastline as the hand-drawn map in the kitchen.
> - **Weather:** steady light rain, low cloud and mist in the distance. Soft, even, overcast
>   light with no sun and no harsh contrast. The sky must still show cloud detail, not white.
> - **Camera:** standing at a ground-floor window, eye height about 1.6 m, looking straight out.
>   Level horizon about 45% of the way up the image. Natural 35mm look, everything sharp.
> - **Format:** the widest landscape format you can make (21:9 or wider). The extra width
>   matters: visitors look at the window from different angles in the 3D room.
> - **Never include:** raindrops or streaks on glass, window frames, people, animals, cars,
>   text, signs, logos, or bright sunset colours.

**If ChatGPT adds a window frame or raindrops on glass anyway:** "Same view, but as if the window
weren't there: no frame, no glass, no drops on glass. Rain only in the air."

**If the sky blows out to white:** "Keep the same scene but bring the sky down a little so the
grey clouds have visible shape."

## The weather set (visitors will change the weather)

Visitors will be able to turn a barometer on the kitchen wall to change the weather outside, and
the weather they choose shapes what Gemma suggests. So we need **the same view in five weathers**.

**This is the most important rule: the framing must be identical in every version.** Same
horizon, same garden wall, same bay, same headlands, all in the same place, so the images can
swap and cross-fade without anything jumping. Only the weather and light change. After the
rainy version is right, use this pattern for each of the others:

> "Now the **exact same view**: identical composition, horizon, wall, fields, bay and headlands,
> nothing moved, but [weather]."

| File | Barometer reading | [weather] |
|---|---|---|
| `14-window-rain.png` | Rain | (the main prompt above) |
| `15-window-fair.png` | Fair | "a clear, warm summer afternoon: blue sky with a few high clouds, sunlight on the fields, the sea bright blue-green and sparkling, everything dry" |
| `16-window-change.png` | Change | "a soft misty morning: sea fog drifting in the bay, headlands half hidden, pale pearly light, still and quiet" |
| `17-window-stormy.png` | Stormy | "a wild winter storm: dark heavy cloud, wind bending the grass, white-capped waves breaking on the headlands, rain driving sideways" |
| `18-window-very-dry.png` | Very Dry | "a hot, still, hazy late-summer day: golden dry grass, heat haze over the bay, pale sky, the sea flat and glassy" |

**Checking them:** put two side by side and flick between them. If the wall, the bay or the
headlands move, ask: "The composition shifted. Redo it matching the rainy version exactly."

## Optional extra (nice for later)

> "The exact same view at dusk, half an hour later: rain easing, the sea darker, two or three
> warm lit cottage windows far off across the bay."

That would let the room have an evening version later.
